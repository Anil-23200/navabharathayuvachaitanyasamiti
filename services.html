Service Details

A closer look at how each service makes a difference in people's lives

Education & Skill Building

Our education services are designed on the foundation of Vivekananda's vision of "Man-Making Education" — education that builds character alongside intellect. We don't just teach subjects; we build people.

Services include free weekend coaching (Classes 6–12), competitive exam preparation, digital skills training, spoken English classes, vocational training for women, and scholarship counseling.

  • Weekend tuition — Mathematics, Science, Telugu, English
  • EAMCET / SSC / Group exam preparation
  • Spoken English & communication skills
  • Basic computer & digital literacy
  • Scholarship & career guidance sessions

Health & Community Welfare

Health is a fundamental right. Our health services bring quality medical attention to doorsteps in rural and underserved communities, partnering with qualified doctors and government healthcare agencies.

We believe in preventive healthcare — educating communities on hygiene, nutrition, and disease prevention alongside providing curative services at our camps.

  • Free general medical consultation
  • Blood pressure & diabetes screening
  • Eye care & dental health camps
  • Women's health awareness sessions
  • Blood donation facilitation & awareness
