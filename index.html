Who We Are

Nava Bharatha Yuva Chaitanya Samiti is a registered youth trust founded on the transformative ideology of Swami Vivekananda — that every young Indian carries within themselves the power to uplift themselves and their society.

The name itself reflects our vision: Nava Bharatha (New India), Yuva (Youth), Chaitanya (Awakening / Consciousness), Samiti (Organisation). We believe a truly new India rises when its youth awakens — in mind, character, and spirit.

Through education, social service, cultural programs, and youth leadership initiatives, we strive to build a generation of confident, compassionate, and service-oriented citizens.

Our Core Philosophy

Rooted in the timeless wisdom of Swami Vivekananda — three pillars that guide every step of our work

📚

Man-Making Education

Vivekananda believed education is not the mere accumulation of knowledge, but the manifestation of perfection already in man. We develop character alongside intellect.

🤝

Karma Yoga — Selfless Service

"Serve man as God." We translate this into action through free education camps, health drives, community service and rural outreach — serving without expectation.

Youth Empowerment

Vivekananda saw youth as the greatest force of a nation. We awaken that potential through leadership training, skill development, and spiritual grounding.

Our Programs

Transforming lives through education, health, culture, and community service

📖

Free Education Camps

Providing free tuition, career guidance, and competitive exam coaching to underprivileged students in rural and semi-urban areas.

🏥

Health & Wellness Camps

Organizing free medical check-up camps, blood donation drives, and awareness campaigns on health and hygiene in underserved communities.

🎭

Cultural & Spiritual Programs

Celebrating Indian culture through Vivekananda Jayanti, Yoga Day, and cultural events that reconnect youth with their heritage and values.
