Our Story

Nava Bharatha Yuva Chaitanya Samiti was born from a deep conviction — that the youth of India hold within themselves an immense, largely untapped power. This power, when awakened through education, values, and selfless service, can transform not just individuals but entire communities.

Founded in 2019, our samiti draws its every breath from the philosophy of Swami Vivekananda, who said: "The youth of India are my hope." We took that hope and made it our mission.

We are a team of volunteers, educators, social workers, and passionate youth who believe that serving one's community is the highest form of worship. Our work spans education, health, culture, and environment — always with the spirit of Karma Yoga at its heart.

The name encapsulates our dream: Nava Bharatha (New India) + Yuva (Youth) + Chaitanya (Awakening) + Samiti (Community Organisation). A new India rises when its youth awakens.

Vision & Mission

🔭

Our Vision

A new India where every young citizen is awakened to their inner potential — fearless, compassionate, and committed to the service of all. A society where Swami Vivekananda's ideal of "Daridra Narayana Seva" — serving God in the form of the poor — is lived, not just preached.

🎯

Our Mission

To build the character and capacity of Indian youth through man-making education, practical Vedanta, and hands-on community service — so that they become the architects of a just, compassionate, and prosperous Nava Bharatha.

Our Core Values

💪

Strength & Fearlessness

"Strength is Life, Weakness is Death." We cultivate inner strength and courage in everything we do.

🙏

Selfless Service

Karma Yoga — action without attachment to reward. We serve because it is our dharma, not for recognition.

🌟

Universal Brotherhood

Every human being is divine. We treat every person we serve with dignity, love, and equality.

📖

Character Education

True education builds character first, knowledge second. We are committed to man-making, not just career-making.

🇮🇳

Love for Motherland

Inspired by Vivekananda's deep love for India — her culture, her people, and her eternal spiritual wisdom.

🔥

Spirit of Inquiry

"Do not believe a thing because I have said it. Think for yourself." We encourage questioning, learning, and growth.

Our Journey

From a small group of dedicated youth to a growing movement of awakening

2019

🌱 Foundation

Nava Bharatha Yuva Chaitanya Samiti is formally registered as a trust, beginning with a core team of 15 passionate youth volunteers committed to Vivekananda's vision.

2020

📚 First Education Camp

Launched our first free education camp serving 60 students from low-income families, providing tuition in Science, Mathematics, and English.

2021

🏥 Health Outreach Begins

Organized our first free medical check-up camp in a rural area, providing health services to over 200 villagers. Also launched our first blood donation drive.

2022

🎭 Vivekananda Jayanti Celebration

Organized a grand Vivekananda Jayanti (National Youth Day) celebration with cultural events, essay competitions, and a youth leadership summit attended by 300+ youth.

2023

🌳 Green India Initiative

Planted 1,000 trees across villages and schools. Expanded our education program to 3 additional villages. Reached 500+ youth members milestone.

2024

🚀 Growing Stronger

Expanded programs to include skill development workshops, digital literacy camps, and women empowerment initiatives. Our movement continues to grow.
