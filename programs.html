Program 01

Free Education & Tuition Camps

Empowering underprivileged students through free quality education — because every child deserves a chance to shine.

About This Program

Our Free Education & Tuition program provides quality coaching to students from Classes 6–12 who cannot afford private tuition. Volunteer teachers from diverse educational backgrounds come together every weekend to teach Mathematics, Science, Telugu, English, and Social Studies.

We also offer career guidance sessions, competitive exam orientation (EAMCET, SSC, Group exams), and personality development workshops — completely free of charge.

📅 Frequency Every Saturday & Sunday
⏰ Timing 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
👥 Beneficiaries 200+ Students
📍 Location Multiple Centers

Key Highlights

  • ✅ Free study materials & stationery provided
  • ✅ Experienced volunteer teachers
  • ✅ Career counseling sessions
  • ✅ Mock tests & exam preparation
  • ✅ Life skills & personality development

Program 02

Free Health & Medical Camps

Bringing quality healthcare to doorsteps — because good health is a right, not a privilege.

About This Program

In collaboration with qualified doctors and healthcare professionals, we organize free medical check-up camps in rural and semi-urban communities. We provide free consultations, basic diagnostics, medicines, and critical case referrals.

Specialized camps focus on eye care, dental hygiene, diabetes and blood pressure screening, skin health, and women's reproductive health awareness.

📅 Frequency Monthly Camps
🩺 Services Consultation + Medicines
👥 Reached 1,000+ Patients
📍 Area Rural & Village Areas

Key Highlights

  • ✅ Free medical consultation by qualified doctors
  • ✅ Free medicines distributed
  • ✅ Blood pressure & sugar screening
  • ✅ Eye & dental check-up camps
  • ✅ Women's health awareness sessions

Program 03

Blood Donation Drives

One donation saves three lives — and every life matters. Join us in making this gift of life.

About This Program

Our blood donation drives mobilize youth volunteers and the broader community to voluntarily donate blood at camps organized in partnership with local government hospitals and blood banks. We create a spirit of community responsibility around blood donation.

Each drive is preceded by awareness sessions on the importance, safety, and eligibility criteria of blood donation, helping overcome fears and misconceptions.

📅 Frequency Quarterly Drives
🩸 Units Collected 500+ Units
🏥 Partner Govt. Blood Banks
🏆 Impact 1,500+ Lives Saved

Key Highlights

  • ✅ Voluntary blood donation camps
  • ✅ Pre-donation health screening
  • ✅ Refreshments & certificate for donors
  • ✅ Awareness sessions on blood donation
  • ✅ Emergency blood support network

Program 04

Cultural & Spiritual Programs

Reconnecting youth with India's timeless spiritual heritage through vibrant celebrations and cultural events.

About This Program

We celebrate India's rich cultural and spiritual heritage through annual events centered around Swami Vivekananda's philosophy. Our flagship celebration is the Vivekananda Jayanti on January 12 — National Youth Day — featuring lectures, cultural performances, essay and debate competitions, and exhibitions.

Other events include International Yoga Day, Independence Day celebrations, and community cultural festivals that bring together youth from all backgrounds.

🎤 Key Event Vivekananda Jayanti
📅 When January 12 (National Youth Day)
👥 Attendance 300+ Youth Per Event
🏆 Competitions Essay, Debate, Elocution

Key Highlights

  • ✅ Annual Vivekananda Jayanti grand event
  • ✅ International Yoga Day celebration
  • ✅ Cultural performances & exhibitions
  • ✅ Youth competitions with prizes
  • ✅ Spiritual discourse & meditation sessions

Program 05

Green India — Tree Plantation Drive

Nurturing the planet we live on — one sapling at a time, one volunteer at a time.

About This Program

In the spirit of environmental responsibility, we organize mass tree plantation drives across schools, villages, roadsides, and public spaces. Each volunteer adopts a sapling and takes responsibility for nurturing it — creating long-term accountability for the environment.

Our "Green Warrior" initiative has seen over 1,000 trees planted and maintained across 5 villages. We focus on native species that support local biodiversity and provide shade, fruits, and oxygen to the community.

🌳 Trees Planted 1,000+
🏘️ Villages Covered 5 Villages
🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Volunteers 200+ Green Warriors
📅 Season Monsoon Drives (July–Aug)

Key Highlights

  • ✅ Mass plantation in schools & villages
  • ✅ Volunteer tree adoption program
  • ✅ Focus on native species
  • ✅ Monthly follow-up & monitoring
  • ✅ Environment awareness rallies

Program 06

Youth Leadership & Skill Development

Building tomorrow's leaders today — with courage, confidence, and Vivekananda's spirit of self-reliance.

About This Program

Conducting free workshops and seminars on leadership, communication, time management, goal setting, and self-confidence — all rooted in Vivekananda's philosophy of inner strength. Sessions are conducted by experienced professionals, educators, and social leaders.

Our digital literacy component bridges the technology gap by teaching basic computer skills, internet safety, and digital tools to rural youth and women — empowering them for the modern economy.

🎓 Age Group Youth aged 16–30
💻 Includes Digital Literacy
📅 Frequency Monthly Workshops
👥 Participants 150+ Youth

Key Highlights

  • ✅ Leadership & communication workshops
  • ✅ Digital literacy for rural youth
  • ✅ Skill development for women
  • ✅ Guest talks by achievers
  • ✅ Certificate of participation

Program 07

Community Service & Disaster Relief

When disaster strikes, we respond — with compassion, speed, and the spirit of Karma Yoga.

About This Program

We mobilize volunteers swiftly during floods, cyclones, and other crises to distribute food, clothing, medicines, and essential relief materials to affected families. We coordinate with local authorities, other NGOs, and government agencies to maximize impact and avoid duplication.

Beyond crisis response, our regular community service activities include cleanliness drives, awareness campaigns, and support to elderly and differently-abled individuals in the community.

⚡ Response Time Within 24 Hours
🎁 Relief Items Food, Clothes, Medicine
🏘️ Families Helped 500+ Families
🤝 Partners Govt. + NGOs

Key Highlights

  • ✅ 24-hour disaster response team
  • ✅ Food & essential kit distribution
  • ✅ Cleanliness & sanitation drives
  • ✅ Support for elderly & differently-abled
  • ✅ Coordination with government agencies
