Who Was Swami Vivekananda?

Born as Narendranath Datta in Calcutta on January 12, 1863, Swami Vivekananda was one of the most powerful spiritual figures India has ever produced. A disciple of the great mystic Sri Ramakrishna Paramahamsa, he carried his guru's universal message of love, tolerance, and the divinity of every human soul to the entire world.

At the Parliament of the World's Religions in Chicago in 1893, his opening words — "Sisters and Brothers of America" — brought the audience to its feet. He went on to place Indian philosophy and spirituality firmly on the world stage.

But Vivekananda was not merely a spiritual teacher — he was a fierce patriot, a visionary social reformer, and the greatest champion of India's youth. He saw poverty as a form of irreligion and called on every Indian to serve the poor as God.

He founded the Ramakrishna Mission in 1897 to combine spiritual development with practical social service — a model that continues to inspire millions, including our samiti.

"I am proud to belong to a nation which has sheltered the persecuted and the refugees of all religions and all nations of the earth." — Swami Vivekananda, Chicago, 1893

Core Teachings We Live By

The four pillars of Vivekananda's philosophy that shape our every action and aspiration

📚

Man-Making Education

"We want that education by which character is formed, strength of mind is increased, the intellect is expanded, and by which one can stand on one's own feet." Vivekananda rejected rote learning and called for education that builds courage, self-reliance, and moral character. This is the education we promote through our programs.

🤲

Karma Yoga — Path of Action

"Karma Yoga is the attainment of freedom through action." Every act of service, performed without selfish motive, purifies the heart and uplifts the soul. We embody Karma Yoga through our free medical camps, education drives, blood donations, and relief work — service as spiritual practice.

🕯️

Practical Vedanta

Vivekananda took the abstract philosophy of Vedanta — that the Divine dwells in every being — and made it intensely practical: "Daridra Narayana Seva" — seeing God in the face of the poor and serving accordingly. Every person we help is an opportunity to worship the living God.

⚡

Strength & Self-Confidence

"The greatest sin is to think yourself weak." Vivekananda thundered against cowardice and self-deprecation. He called on Indian youth to be lions, not sheep — to stand up, be bold, take responsibility. Our leadership programs are built entirely on this philosophy of inner strength.

🌍

Universal Brotherhood

"We believe not only in universal toleration, but we accept all religions as true." Vivekananda's vision transcended barriers of caste, creed, religion, and nationality. Our samiti welcomes youth of all backgrounds and serves communities without discrimination of any kind.

🔥

National Consciousness & Patriotism

"Say, 'this is my country, and every Indian is my brother.'" Vivekananda's love for India was deep and passionate. He urged youth to study India's great past, serve her present needs, and build her glorious future. We celebrate this national spirit through our cultural and awareness programs.

The Four Paths of Yoga

Vivekananda taught that there are four paths to spiritual realization — and every personality can find its way

🧠

Jnana Yoga

Path of Knowledge & Wisdom

For the philosophical and intellectual temperament — realizing the Self through the power of discrimination and inquiry. "Know thyself" is the core practice.

💛

Bhakti Yoga

Path of Love & Devotion

For the emotional and devotional heart — channeling love towards God or the Ideal. All love is, in its essence, love of the Divine.

🤝

Karma Yoga

Path of Selfless Action

For the active and energetic personality — doing one's duty without attachment to results. Every act becomes a form of worship.

🧘

Raja Yoga

Path of Meditation & Self-Control

For the scientific and meditative mind — disciplining the body, breath, and mind through concentration and meditation to realize inner divinity.
